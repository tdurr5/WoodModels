Measuring snaps to endpoints, midpoints and edges within a few pixels, otherwise to the face you click. With a part selected, the corners of its grey outline box snap too (handy for the square corner above an angled cut), and moving along the part's length, width or thickness (or level/plumb) locks onto that line with a dashed guide. With a part selected, only that part can be measured, so joints with neighbouring parts don't get in the way.

Link to a part: the address bar updates as you select, e.g. #part=Leg_Rear.
